Our home is a vibrant, blue marble and the only known world capable of supporting life. Earth’s perfect distance from the Sun allows for liquid water to exist on its surface, while its nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere and protective magnetic field create a stable environment for a vast diversity of ecosystems to thrive.
